Common Questions

Why does the app ask for microphone permission?

The app uses microphone input only for real-time frequency and spectrum analysis.

Does the app record audio?

No. The app analyzes audio in real time and does not record, store, or upload microphone audio.

How do I manage my subscription?

You can manage or cancel subscriptions from your Apple ID subscription settings.
